Agentic AI Builder

A 4-week course that takes you from “what is an AI?” to building AI helpers that do real work. These notes explain everything in plain language, with everyday examples.

How to use these notes Read each lesson before or after class. Try the small activities on the page, do the exercise at the end, then take the quick quiz. No technical background is needed. Every week ends with a project, marked in red.
